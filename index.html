BMI Calculator

Body mass index from your height and weight.

Your BMI

—

—

Formula & explanation

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m²)

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is obesity.

Stress Calculator

Mechanical / engineering stress from an applied force and cross-sectional area.

Mechanical Stress (σ)

—

—

Formula & explanation

σ = F / A

σ is stress, F is the applied force, and A is the cross-sectional area it acts on. Stress is reported in pascals (Pa), where 1 Pa = 1 N/m².

Strain Calculator

Deformation relative to original length.

Use the same length unit for both fields — the result is unit-independent.

Strain (ε)

—

—

—

—

Formula & explanation

ε = ΔL / L₀

ε is strain, ΔL is the change in length, and L₀ is the original length. A positive strain is tensile (stretching); a negative strain is compressive (shortening).
